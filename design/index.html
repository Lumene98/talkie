Chat controls

  1. Set a username and select a person to open a direct chat.
  2. Hold the talk button or Space to transmit. Release to let the other person speak.
  3. Each audio message includes its transcript. Use Play to hear a browser-generated demo voice or Save to download the transcript.

This is an interactive design prototype. Audio and captions use scripted examples; your microphone is never accessed.
